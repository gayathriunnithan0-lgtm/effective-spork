Brain-teaser of the day

A bat and a ball cost ₹110 in total. The bat costs ₹100 more than the ball.
How much does the ball cost?

₹5. Not ₹10. If the ball cost ₹10, the bat would be ₹110 and the total ₹120. The ball is ₹5 and the bat is ₹105 — which is exactly ₹100 more.

most adults get this wrong!

Why kids actually enjoy it

Four rules we never break. They are the whole method, really.

"He used to hide his maths notebook. Now he shows me the puzzle he solved."

— the kind of line we work for

  1. Formulas are derived, never dictated

    Your child works out why the formula exists, once. After that, recalling it is easy — and so is surviving a question that twists it.

  2. Every chapter opens with a puzzle

    Before the definition, before the notation, there is a puzzle that makes the idea obvious. The textbook language then feels like a description of something they already know.

  3. Mistakes are the syllabus

    A wrong answer earns a two-minute mini-lesson, not a red face. We keep a running list of brilliant mistakes and revisit them before exams.

  4. Parents get receipts, every month

    A short report naming exactly what your child has mastered and what still needs work — with sample questions, not just grades out of twenty.

Questions parents ask

Are the classes online or in person?

Both. Online classes run over video with a shared whiteboard, and in-person options are available. Tell us your preference while booking the trial.

How big are the batches?

Batches are capped at five students, so every child attempts every question in every class — nobody hides at the back.

Which syllabus do you follow?

CBSE and ICSE, aligned to NCERT for classes 6, 7 and 8. If your school uses a different textbook, we build the plan around that instead.

What if my child is already far behind?

Most students start behind. The first two classes are a diagnosis: we locate the exact gaps, list them for you, and fill them in the right order.

How will I know it is working?

A short monthly report showing what has been mastered and what needs work, plus a call after the first month to talk it through.

Is there any long-term commitment?

None. Classes are billed monthly, and you can pause or stop with a week's notice. No admission fees, no contracts.
